# First 30-Day Non-Goals

The following are explicitly outside the first 30-day wallet MVP.

## Fiat Rails

Not implementing:

- Bank transfers
- Fiat deposits
- Fiat withdrawals
- Payment processors

## Leveraged Trading

Not implementing:

- Futures
- Perpetuals
- Margin
- Leverage

## Institutional Custody

Not implementing:

- Institutional custody
- Institutional account management
- Enterprise treasury

## Broad Blockchain Coverage

Not attempting to support every blockchain.

Initial support is limited to:

- EVM
- Solana
- Bitcoin

## Exchange

The first 30 days do not attempt to launch a regulated public
cryptocurrency exchange.

## Other Explicitly Deferred Features

- Full trading engine
- Fiat exchange
- Institutional custody
- Large-scale chain expansion
- Advanced DeFi aggregation
